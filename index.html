Frequently asked questions

Can I book an online appointment?

Yes. Call or message Tax Clinic and ask for an online appointment when you book.

What if I need help with both income tax and GST?

Mention both when you contact the office so they can advise how to schedule your appointment.

How can I find out what it will cost?

Tell the office which service and filing period you need help with. You can ask about fees and turnaround time before you book.

How should I share my documents?

Ask the office which records they need and how they prefer to receive them. You can use the WhatsApp button to check before sending anything.
